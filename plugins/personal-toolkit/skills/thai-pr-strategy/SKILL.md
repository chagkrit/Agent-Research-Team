# Thai PR Strategy Advisor

## Why this skill exists

Thai PR practice has a gap: 97.5% of practitioners already use AI and 74.4%
call themselves "digital-first," but most still measure success the old way
— Output volume and Advertising Value Equivalency (AVE) — instead of the
Outcome/Impact-based standards the rest of the world adopted years ago. This
skill exists to close that gap: when someone asks for PR advice, push them
toward the modern framework and modern channel mix, backed by the actual
survey data, instead of defaulting to "send more press releases and count
clippings."

Source: "Public RelationSHIFT: Exploring PR in the Digital Age 2026,"
Moonshot Digital + Chulalongkorn University Faculty of Communication Arts,
survey of 222 Thai PR professionals across government, private sector,
civil society, and agencies. Cite it this way when referencing the data.

## How to use this skill

1. **Diagnose where they are.** Ask (or infer from what they've described)
   how they currently measure PR success. If they mention AVE, "มูลค่าสื่อ,"
   clipping counts, or reach/impressions as the *primary* KPI, that's the
   anti-pattern this research flags — name it directly and explain why it
   undersells the work, then move them toward outcome-based KPIs.
2. **Build the measurement plan on AMEC, not AVE.** See
   `references/amec-vs-ave.md` for the full Output/Outtake/Outcome/Impact
   breakdown, a worked KPI tree example, and ready-to-use talking points for
   justifying the switch to a skeptical client or finance team.
3. **Plan the channel mix with PESO, grounded in Thai-market data.** See
   `references/channel-data-thailand.md` for which channels are most
   *influential* (brand social, KOL, micro-communities, online news,
   traditional media) versus which channels actually convert best in
   practice (Facebook, TikTok, website, Instagram, LINE) — these are
   different lists, and conflating them is a common mistake. Channel choice
   should follow brand/product type, not a generic "best platform" list.
4. **Choose the right voice for the message.** Influencers are the fastest
   lever for short-term reach, but durability comes from a different mix:
   content consistency, fact-referenced material, real-consumer KOC
   reviews, and — disproportionately effective in this research — the
   CEO/Founder speaking directly. Default recommendation: pair
   influencer/KOL reach with at least one owned, repeatable, fact-based
   format and consider a founder-led component, rather than treating
   influencer spend as a complete strategy.
5. **Stress-test the plan against the 9 emerging trends.** See
   `references/nine-trends.md`. Don't list all nine at someone by rote —
   pick the 1-3 that are actually load-bearing for their situation (e.g. a
   brand worried about a crisis needs Immediate-Response Crisis +
   Hybrid Newsroom; a brand obsessing over press-release volume needs Slow
   PR; anyone asking about SEO/content discovery in 2026 needs GEO).
6. **Be honest about what's Thailand-specific vs. universal.** AMEC and
   PESO are global standards this research confirms Thai practice is
   lagging on. The channel-ranking data and the AVE/measurement-maturity
   finding are Thailand-specific (222 Thai respondents) — flag this when a
   recommendation leans on the local data versus the global framework, so
   the advice doesn't overgeneralize to other markets.

## Quick reference: the 9 trends (one-liners)

1. **Hybrid Newsroom** — distribute simultaneously to traditional media,
   creators, and influencers; don't run separate workflows.
2. **AI-Powered Social Listening** — sentiment + reporting tooling becomes
   table stakes, not a nice-to-have.
3. **Measurement Transformation** — AMEC + outcome-based KPIs become a
   hiring/contract requirement; AVE's role shrinks over ~3 years.
4. **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** — optimize so AI answer engines
   (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, etc.) surface and correctly represent the
   brand, alongside traditional SEO.
5. **Founder Lead Branding** — leadership visibly communicating vision,
   tied to product/service, as a differentiator.
6. **Creative Thinking Returns** — the unexpected, human idea AI can't
   generate becomes the differentiator again.
7. **Immediate-Response Crisis** — brands are expected to respond to
   incidents/drama in near real-time to prevent escalation.
8. **Slow PR** — fewer, better-considered campaigns over high-volume
   low-value press releases; optimize for long-term Impact.
9. **Pay-to-Play AI Results** — expect paid placement/advertising inside AI
   chat answers, analogous to paid search.

Full detail, implications, and suggested action items for each are in
`references/nine-trends.md` — read it when a recommendation needs to go
deeper than the one-liner.
